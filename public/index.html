How to choose between solar companies in Austin

Six checks that separate a dependable installer from a risky one. Ask each company you shortlist.

  • Austin Energy participating contractor

    Only participating contractors can file for the $2,500 rebate. Ask for confirmation in writing.

  • Texas electrical contractor licence

    Solar installers in Texas need a state electrical contractor licence from TDLR. Look the number up before signing.

  • NABCEP-certified staff

    NABCEP is the main professional credential for solar design and installation in North America.

  • Their own installation crew

    Ask who will be on your roof. Subcontracted crews are common with national brands and make accountability harder.

  • Workmanship warranty of ten years or more

    Panel makers cover the equipment. The installer's warranty covers leaks, wiring and labour, and it is only worth what the company behind it is.

  • Price per watt in the quote

    Divide the total price by system size. It is the one figure that lets you compare quotes of different sizes fairly.

Questions about Austin solar companies

How many solar companies are there in Austin?

This directory lists 29 solar companies that serve Austin, of which 17 are based in the Austin area. Austin Energy's own list of participating solar contractors is longer, at roughly 65 to 72 firms, because it includes every contractor registered for its rebate programme.

Which Austin Texas solar companies qualify for the Austin Energy rebate?

Only installers on Austin Energy's participating solar contractor list can apply for the rebate on your behalf. The list changes, so check it on austinenergy.com or ask the installer to confirm in writing before you sign.

What do solar power companies in Austin, TX charge?

Published averages for Austin range from about $2.22 to $3.32 per watt, with SolarReviews putting the 2026 average at $2.84 per watt. Local installers are often reported to be cheaper than national brands for comparable equipment. Always compare quotes on price per watt.

Is a local or a national solar company better?

Local companies usually have their own crews, know Austin Energy's permitting and interconnection process, and are easier to reach for service. National companies offer more lease and PPA options. Whichever you choose, check who actually installs the system and who honours the workmanship warranty.

Do I need a battery with solar in Austin?

No. A grid-tied system works without one. A battery keeps selected circuits running during an outage, which matters to many Central Texas homeowners after the 2021 and 2023 winter storms, but it adds substantially to the price.

How long does a solar installation take in Austin?

The work on the roof usually takes one to three days. Design, permitting, Austin Energy's letter of intent and the final inspection add several weeks before the system is switched on.
