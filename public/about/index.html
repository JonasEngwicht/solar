Solar companies in Austin / About

How this directory works

An independent list of Austin Texas solar companies, built from public information.

Who is listed

Companies are included when public sources show that they install solar in Austin and give enough detail to describe them fairly. Companies based in the Austin area are shown first, followed by Central Texas, statewide and national providers.

Where the information comes from

Founding years, addresses, phone numbers, services and ratings come from company websites and from public profiles on EnergySage, SolarReviews, EcoWatch, ConsumerAffairs, Solar Power World and Google. Ratings are quoted with their source and are not our own.

Companies marked as not yet reviewed come from Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors, copied on 17 November 2026. For those, the directory shows only what that list states: address, phone number and whether they install residential or commercial systems. Details are added as each company is reviewed.

How price levels are set

Most installers do not publish prices. A price level appears only where a company publishes per-watt pricing or states its own positioning, and each profile says what the level is based on. Everything else is marked as priced by quote.

Independence

Listings are free. No company pays to be included or to appear higher, and the order is not a ranking of quality.

Corrections

Details change. If you represent a listed company and something is wrong or out of date, the listing will be corrected on request. Always confirm prices, licences and warranty terms with the company itself.
