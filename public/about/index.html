How this directory works

An independent list of Austin Texas solar companies, built from public information.

Who is listed

Companies are included when public sources show that they install solar in Austin and give enough detail to describe them fairly. Companies based in the Austin area are shown first, followed by Central Texas, statewide and national providers.

Where the information comes from

Founding years, addresses, phone numbers, services and ratings come from company websites and from public profiles on EnergySage, SolarReviews, EcoWatch, ConsumerAffairs, Solar Power World and Google. Ratings are quoted with their source and are not our own.

Rebate status comes from Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors, copied on 17 November 2026; 55 of the 69 companies here were on it. Companies marked as not yet reviewed come from that list. For those, the directory shows only what that list states: address, phone number and whether they install residential or commercial systems. Details are added as each company is reviewed.

How price levels are set

Most installers do not publish prices. A price level appears only where a company publishes per-watt pricing or states its own positioning, and each profile says what the level is based on. Everything else is marked as priced by quote.

Independence

Listings are free. No company pays to be included or to appear higher, and the order is not a ranking of quality.

How it is kept up to date

Rates and program rules on the guide pages are checked against the pages of Austin Energy and the other utilities, and each guide lists its sources and the date of the last check. Every page shows the date it last changed. Worked examples come from one published model, the same one behind the solar calculator, so the figures on different pages agree.

Corrections

Details change. If you represent a listed company and something is wrong or out of date, write to contact@austintexassolar.com and the listing will be corrected. Always confirm prices, licenses and warranty terms with the company itself.

Who runs it

Austin Texas Solar is a private, independent project and is not affiliated with Austin Energy or any listed company. The operator is named in the legal notice.
