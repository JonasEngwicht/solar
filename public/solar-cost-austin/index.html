The Austin Energy solar rebate

Austin Energy pays residential customers a flat $2,500 rebate for a qualifying solar installation. To receive it you must:

  1. Be an Austin Energy residential customer.
  2. Complete Austin Energy's online solar education course.
  3. Hire an Austin Energy participating solar contractor, who applies on your behalf.
  4. Receive a letter of intent from Austin Energy before installation starts.
  5. Pass Austin Energy's final inspection.

Austin Energy recommends getting at least three estimates from different participating contractors. The current list is on austinenergy.com.

Value of Solar instead of net metering

Austin Energy does not use traditional net metering. It credits the electricity your system produces at a set Value of Solar rate, which installers reported at about 9.9 cents per kWh in 2026. The rate is reviewed periodically, so check Austin Energy's current rate schedule before estimating your savings.

Federal tax credit

The 30% federal tax credit for homeowner-owned residential systems ended on December 31, 2025, according to several installer and industry sources. Leased systems and power purchase agreements can still benefit from a separate credit claimed by the provider. Treat any 2026 quote that subtracts 30% for a purchased home system with caution, and confirm your situation with a tax professional.

Why quotes from Austin solar companies differ

  • Equipment: premium panels and microinverters cost more than standard ones.
  • Company type: national brands are reported to charge roughly 15 to 25% more than local installers.
  • Roof: steep, tiled or shaded roofs take more labour.
  • Hail: Central Texas sees several significant hail events a year, so ask about panel hail ratings.
  • Batteries: storage adds substantially to the price but provides backup during outages.
