With a lease or PPA the company owns the panels. In 2026 that is the only way homeowners still get value from the federal tax credit. Austin Energy does not allow third-party PPAs in its service territory, so there the choice is buying, a loan or a lease. A lease runs 20 to 25 years and has to pass to a buyer if you sell. Before you sign with a large provider

  • Freedom Forever filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection on 15 April 2026 and keeps operating under court supervision. Ask who will honor the workmanship warranty.
  • Texas Attorney General announced in April 2026 an investigation into the sales practices of several residential installers, including Freedom Forever and Sunrun. An investigation is not a finding of wrongdoing.
  • Sunrun and Palmetto give their longest warranties to lease and PPA customers. If you buy, check which terms apply to you.
  • Palmetto does not install itself: a partner company in its network does the work, so ask which one.
  • Longhorn Solar was not on Austin Energy's participating contractor list when we checked, so it cannot file for the $4,000 rebate.
