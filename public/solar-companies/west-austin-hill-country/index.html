Questions

How many solar companies are based in West Austin and the Hill Country?

This directory lists 8 solar companies with an office in West Austin, West Lake Hills, Rollingwood, Bee Cave, Lakeway, Oak Hill and Dripping Springs. 6 of them were on Austin Energy's participating contractor list when we checked on 17 November 2026.

Can I get the Austin Energy solar rebate in West Austin and the Hill Country?

Austin Energy lists Bee Cave, Lakeway, Rollingwood, West Lake Hills and the Village of the Hills in its service area, so the $4,000 rebate is available there. Farther west many homes are served by Pedernales Electric Cooperative, which has no comparable solar rebate.

Do I have to hire a solar company from West Austin and the Hill Country?

No. Most Austin-area installers work across the whole metro area, so compare quotes from at least three companies whatever their office address. What matters for the rebate is your utility and whether the installer is a participating contractor.
