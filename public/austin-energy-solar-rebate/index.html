The Austin Energy solar rebate: $4,000 and how to get it

Austin Energy raised its home solar rebate from $2,500 to $4,000 on 1 July 2026. Here is who qualifies and the order in which things have to happen.

In short

  • $4,000 for a new home system of at least 3 kW, paid by check after the final inspection.
  • Only an Austin Energy participating contractor can apply, and it applies for you.
  • The confirmation letter has to arrive before installation starts.

The steps in order

  1. Take Austin Energy's Solar Rebate Quiz. Have your contact details, Austin Energy account number and address ready. You get a rebate reference number at the end.
  2. Get quotes and hire a participating contractor. Austin Energy recommends at least three quotes. Give the reference number to the contractor you choose; they apply on your behalf.
  3. Sign two forms. The Residential Interconnection Agreement, needed for every home system, and the Customer Agreement Form, needed for rebated systems.
  4. Wait for the Solar Rebate Confirmation Letter. Installation must not start before it arrives.
  5. Installation.
  6. Final inspection. After it passes, Austin Energy installs the solar meter and you get permission to operate.
  7. Quality assurance review. The rebate is processed after this last check, and the check is mailed about 7 to 10 business days after approval.

What costs you the rebate

  • Hiring a company that is not on Austin Energy's participating contractor list.
  • Installing before the confirmation letter arrives.
  • A system under 3 kW (DC) or a heavily shaded roof.
  • Living outside Austin Energy's service area. Parts of Round Rock, Leander and Hays County are served by other utilities; see the North Austin and Round Rock and Buda and San Marcos pages.

Other Austin Energy incentives

  • Value of Solar credits of 12.89 cents for every kWh your system produces. See how the Value of Solar rate works.
  • Property tax exemption for the added value of a solar system, administered by the Texas Comptroller.
  • Power Partner Battery, a demand response pilot that pays $500 per new battery plus $75 per kW a year for letting Austin Energy use stored energy at peak times. See solar batteries in Austin.

Questions

How much is the Austin Energy solar rebate?

Austin Energy pays $4,000 for a qualifying new home solar system installed by a participating contractor. The amount was raised from $2,500 on 1 July 2026.

Who can get the Austin Energy solar rebate?

Austin Energy residential customers who install a new system of at least 3 kW (DC) that is mostly free of shading, at the home tied to their Austin Energy account, through a participating solar contractor.

Can I install first and apply for the rebate later?

No. You must receive Austin Energy's Solar Rebate Confirmation Letter before the system is installed, otherwise the system is not eligible.

Is a solar PPA allowed with Austin Energy?

No. Austin Energy states that third-party solar power purchase agreements are not allowed in its service territory.

How long does it take to receive the rebate?

The rebate is paid at the end: after installation, the final inspection and Austin Energy's quality assurance review. Austin Energy says the check is mailed about 7 to 10 business days after that approval.
