When you need a service company

  • The system produces less than it used to, or nothing. Check the monitoring app and the inverter display first and note any error code before you call.
  • Your installer has closed or stopped answering. It happens with national brands as well: Freedom Forever filed for Chapter 11 protection in April 2026. NATiVE Solar says it takes over systems another installer abandoned, and 512 Solar runs a service arm for existing systems.
  • The roof needs replacing. The panels and racking have to come off and go back on.
  • Hail or storm damage. Central Texas sees several significant hail events a year. Have the array inspected along with the roof.

Before you hire

  • Ask your original installer first, if it still exists. The workmanship warranty is tied to that company, and work by someone else can affect it.
  • Let a solar company take the panels off, not the roofer. Disconnecting an array is electrical work.
  • Check your insurance. When a roof is replaced after a covered event such as hail, the policy may pay for removing and reinstalling the panels.
  • Get photos and a written scope. Ask for pictures of every panel before removal and after reinstallation, and for the price per panel.
  • Check the license. Electrical work in Texas needs a state electrical contractor license from TDLR.

Questions

Who repairs solar panels in Austin if my installer went out of business?

10 companies in this directory list repair or maintenance. NATiVE Solar says it takes over systems another installer abandoned, and 512 Solar troubleshoots and repairs existing arrays. Ask any of them whether they service your brand of inverter, and keep your original contract and equipment list at hand.

How much does it cost to remove and reinstall solar panels for a new roof?

EnergySage puts it at about $200 to $300 per panel, which can pass $7,000 for a large system. NuWatt quotes $2,800 to $4,800 as typical in Texas for a 28-panel system. Get the price in writing before the roofer starts.

Does insurance pay for removing solar panels after hail damage?

It can. When the roof is replaced because of a covered event such as hail, homeowner's insurance may cover removal and reinstallation of the panels. Ask your insurer before you sign with a roofer.

Will a repair by another company affect my warranty?

It may. Workmanship warranties are tied to the original installer, so ask that company first if it still exists. Equipment warranties from the panel and inverter makers stay with the equipment.
