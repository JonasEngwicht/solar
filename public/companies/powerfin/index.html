About PowerFin

PowerFin develops, builds and operates solar projects from Austin and San Antonio. It signed a 25-year agreement to sell community solar power to Austin Energy and built and owns the rooftop arrays of CPS Energy's SolarHost programme in San Antonio. It is a developer more than a home installer.

Before you sign with PowerFin

  • Ask whether the company is currently an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies.
  • Ask who installs the system and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
