About HOLT Renewables

HOLT Renewables designs and builds commercial solar, battery storage and EV charging projects across the US from its Austin base. It grew out of Austin's PCI Solar, which HOLT, the San Antonio owner of the HOLT CAT dealership, acquired in 2019. It designed the 12-acre solar canopy that charges CapMetro's electric buses.

Before you sign with HOLT Renewables

  • Ask whether the company is currently an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies.
  • Ask who installs the system and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
