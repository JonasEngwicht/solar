The Austin Energy rebate with ELK Electric

ELK Electric was on Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors when we checked on 17 November 2026. That means it can apply for the $4,000 home solar rebate on your behalf, provided your home has an Austin Energy account, the system is at least 3 kW and the confirmation letter arrives before installation.

The steps, in the order they have to happen, are in how to get the Austin Energy solar rebate. Homes served by Oncor, PEC or another utility follow different rules; see solar incentives by utility.

Where ELK Electric is based

ELK Electric has its office in Austin, which this directory counts under South Austin. Most homes here are Austin Energy customers, so the $4,000 rebate applies when you hire a participating contractor. Austin Energy lists Sunset Valley and Manchaca in its service area besides the City of Austin.

Other installers nearby are on the South Austin page.

Before you sign with ELK Electric

  • Ask the company to confirm in writing that it is still an Austin Energy participating solar contractor.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies. The 2026 Austin average is $2.84 per watt.
  • Ask who installs the system, their own crew or a subcontractor, and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
  • Look up the company's Texas electrical contractor license with TDLR.
