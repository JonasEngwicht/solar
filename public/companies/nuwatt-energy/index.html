The Austin Energy rebate with NuWatt Energy

NuWatt Energy was not on Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors when we checked on 17 November 2026. An Austin Energy customer who hires a company that is not on the list cannot get the $4,000 rebate, so ask the company about its current status before you compare prices.

The steps, in the order they have to happen, are in how to get the Austin Energy solar rebate. Homes served by Oncor, PEC or another utility follow different rules; see solar incentives by utility.

Before you sign with NuWatt Energy

  • Ask whether the company has become an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies. The 2026 Austin average is $2.84 per watt.
  • Ask who installs the system, their own crew or a subcontractor, and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
  • Look up the company's Texas electrical contractor license with TDLR.
