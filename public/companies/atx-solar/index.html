Before you sign with ATX Solar

  • Ask whether the company is currently an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $2,500 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies.
  • Ask who installs the system and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
