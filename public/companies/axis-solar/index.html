Axis Solar

Built the first commercial project under Austin Energy's Solar Standard Offer in 2025; Tesla Powerwall certified.

About Axis Solar

Axis Solar was founded in Austin in 2018 and works mostly on commercial systems. Its team is a Tesla Powerwall Certified Installer and an Enphase Silver Installer. In December 2025 Austin Energy presented its first commercial Solar Standard Offer project, an 812-panel warehouse roof that Axis Solar installed.

Before you sign with Axis Solar

  • Ask whether the company is currently an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies.
  • Ask who installs the system and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
