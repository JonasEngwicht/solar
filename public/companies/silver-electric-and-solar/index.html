The Austin Energy rebate with Silver Electric and Solar

Silver Electric and Solar was on Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors when we checked on 17 November 2026. That means it can apply for the $4,000 home solar rebate on your behalf, provided your home has an Austin Energy account, the system is at least 3 kW and the confirmation letter arrives before installation.

The steps, in the order they have to happen, are in how to get the Austin Energy solar rebate. Homes served by Oncor, PEC or another utility follow different rules; see solar incentives by utility.

Where Silver Electric and Solar is based

Silver Electric and Solar has its office in Leander, which this directory counts under North Austin, Round Rock and Pflugerville. This area is split between utilities. Austin Energy serves North Austin and lists Pflugerville and Cedar Park in its service area. Other homes, including much of Round Rock, are on the Oncor network, where Oncor runs its own Residential Solar Program instead of the Austin Energy rebate. Check the utility named on your bill before you compare quotes.

Before you sign with Silver Electric and Solar

  • Ask the company to confirm in writing that it is still an Austin Energy participating solar contractor.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies. The 2026 Austin average is $2.84 per watt.
  • Ask who installs the system, their own crew or a subcontractor, and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
  • Look up the company's Texas electrical contractor license with TDLR.
