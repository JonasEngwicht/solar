The Austin Energy rebate with The American Power Company

The American Power Company was not on Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors when we checked on 17 November 2026. An Austin Energy customer who hires a company that is not on the list cannot get the $4,000 rebate, so ask the company about its current status before you compare prices.

The steps, in the order they have to happen, are in how to get the Austin Energy solar rebate. Homes served by Oncor, PEC or another utility follow different rules; see solar incentives by utility.

Where The American Power Company is based

The American Power Company has its office in Austin, which this directory counts under West Austin and the Hill Country. Austin Energy lists Bee Cave, Lakeway, Rollingwood, West Lake Hills and the Village of the Hills in its service area, so the $4,000 rebate is available there. Farther west many homes are served by Pedernales Electric Cooperative, which has no comparable solar rebate.

Other installers nearby are on the West Austin and the Hill Country page.

Before you sign with The American Power Company

  • Ask whether the company has become an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies. The 2026 Austin average is $2.84 per watt.
  • Ask who installs the system, their own crew or a subcontractor, and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
  • Look up the company's Texas electrical contractor license with TDLR.
