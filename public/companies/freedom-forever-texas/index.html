About Freedom Forever Texas

Freedom Forever is based in Temecula, California, and was one of the largest US residential installers. It uses subcontracted installers. On 15 April 2026 it filed for Chapter 11 bankruptcy protection in Delaware and keeps operating under court supervision, so ask who will service the system and honour its warranties.

Before you sign with Freedom Forever Texas

  • Ask whether the company is currently an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies.
  • Ask who installs the system and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
