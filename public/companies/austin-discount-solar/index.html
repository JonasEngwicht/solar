The Austin Energy rebate with Austin Discount Solar

Austin Discount Solar was not on Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors when we checked on 17 November 2026. An Austin Energy customer who hires a company that is not on the list cannot get the $4,000 rebate, so ask the company about its current status before you compare prices.

Where Austin Discount Solar is based

Austin Discount Solar has its office in Austin, which this directory counts under North Austin, Round Rock and Pflugerville. This area is split between utilities. Austin Energy serves North Austin and lists Pflugerville and Cedar Park in its service area. Other homes, including much of Round Rock, are on the Oncor network, where Oncor runs its own Residential Solar Program instead of the Austin Energy rebate. Check the utility named on your bill before you compare quotes.

Other installers nearby are on the North Austin, Round Rock and Pflugerville page.

Before you sign with Austin Discount Solar

  • Ask whether the company has become an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies. The 2026 Austin average is $2.84 per watt.
  • Ask who installs the system, their own crew or a subcontractor, and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
  • Look up the company's Texas electrical contractor license with TDLR.
