About ION Solar

ION Solar is headquartered in Provo, Utah, has installed solar since 2013 and lists an Austin office with Austin Energy. On SolarReviews it averages 3.98 out of 5 from more than 1,100 reviews. In BBB complaint responses the company has described a two-year labor warranty on past installations, so ask for the current terms in writing.

Before you sign with ION Solar

  • Ask whether the company is currently an Austin Energy participating solar contractor. Without that, you cannot claim the $4,000 rebate.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies.
  • Ask who installs the system and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
