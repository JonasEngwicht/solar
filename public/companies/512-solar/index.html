The Austin Energy rebate with 512 Solar

512 Solar was on Austin Energy's list of participating solar contractors when we checked on 17 November 2026. That means it can apply for the $4,000 home solar rebate on your behalf, provided your home has an Austin Energy account, the system is at least 3 kW and the confirmation letter arrives before installation.

The steps, in the order they have to happen, are in how to get the Austin Energy solar rebate. Homes served by Oncor, PEC or another utility follow different rules; see solar incentives by utility.

Where 512 Solar is based

512 Solar has its office in Austin, which this directory counts under West Austin and the Hill Country. Austin Energy lists Bee Cave, Lakeway, Rollingwood, West Lake Hills and the Village of the Hills in its service area, so the $4,000 rebate is available there. Farther west many homes are served by Pedernales Electric Cooperative, which has no comparable solar rebate.

Before you sign with 512 Solar

  • Ask the company to confirm in writing that it is still an Austin Energy participating solar contractor.
  • Get the total price and the price per watt in writing, and compare with at least two other Austin solar companies. The 2026 Austin average is $2.84 per watt.
  • Ask who installs the system, their own crew or a subcontractor, and how long the workmanship warranty runs.
  • Look up the company's Texas electrical contractor license with TDLR.
