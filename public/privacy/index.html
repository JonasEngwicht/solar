2. Hosting and server logs

The site is delivered by Cloudflare, Inc. (USA) as our processor under a data processing agreement. To deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes your IP address, the date and time of the request, the page requested and technical browser details. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a secure, working website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). For transfers to the United States, Cloudflare is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and also relies on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

3. No cookies, analytics or tracking

We do not set cookies and do not use analytics or advertising tools. Fonts and the map library are served from our own domain, so loading a page does not contact Google or other third parties.

4. The map (only if you click "Show map")

The interactive map is off until you click "Show map". Only then does your browser load map tiles from the OpenStreetMap Foundation (United Kingdom), which receives your IP address and browser details. Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Your browser remembers this choice in local storage so the map opens on your next visit; you can withdraw it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser. See the OpenStreetMap Foundation privacy policy.

5. Contacting us

If you email us, we use your address and message only to reply, and delete them when they are no longer needed (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR).

6. Links to other websites

Company profiles link to company websites and to sources. Once you follow such a link, that website's own privacy policy applies.

7. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection, and to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 15 to 21 GDPR). You can also complain to a data protection supervisory authority, for example in your country of residence or at our place of business.
