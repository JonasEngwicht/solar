Austin Energy's Value of Solar rate, explained

Austin Energy pays a fixed credit for every kilowatt-hour your panels produce instead of using net metering. Since 1 October 2026 that credit is 12.89 cents for homes.

The current rates

System sizeValue of Solar rate
Under 1 MW-AC (all home systems)12.89 cents per kWh
1 MW-AC and over10.22 cents per kWh

Effective 1 October 2026 and subject to change. Austin Energy updates the rate every three years.

How the credit works

  1. Production: a separate solar meter measures everything your panels produce, and Austin Energy credits all of it at the Value of Solar rate.
  2. Consumption: you pay normal residential rates for all the electricity your home uses, whether it came from your roof or the grid.
  3. Credit: the credit is subtracted from the electric part of your bill. What is left over carries to the next month.

Credits cannot be cashed out or transferred, and they do not reduce water, trash or other city charges. Austin Energy therefore recommends sizing a system to your yearly consumption rather than larger.

A quick example

A system that produces 10,000 kWh in a year earns 10,000 × $0.1289 = $1,289 in credits that year. Real production depends on size, roof direction and shade; Austin Energy points to NREL's free PVWatts calculator for an estimate.

Estimating payback

Austin Energy suggests dividing the net cost of the system by your yearly savings from Value of Solar credits. A 7 kW system at the 2026 Austin average of $2.84 per watt costs $19,880; after the $4,000 rebate that is $15,880. If you finance or lease, include payment increases over the full term. Questions

What is the Austin Energy Value of Solar rate in 2026?

For homes and other systems under 1 MW-AC it is 12.89 cents per kWh, effective 1 October 2026. Systems of 1 MW-AC and more receive 10.22 cents.

Does Austin Energy have net metering?

No. Austin Energy credits all the energy your system produces at the Value of Solar rate and bills all the energy your home uses at its normal rates.

What happens to extra solar credits?

Credits larger than your electric charges carry over to the next month. They cannot be cashed out or transferred and do not apply to water, trash or other city services.
